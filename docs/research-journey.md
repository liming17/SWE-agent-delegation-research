# From an appealing observation to a better control

[← Project overview](../README.md)

## 1. An initial observation

The original isolated study observed nine A-pass/B-fail pairs among 58 Direct-pass pairs. Source-Anchored C succeeded on five of those nine. Textual coding separated omitted and preserved labels.

An appealing interpretation was that delegation caused the selected failures and restored requirements caused their recovery. The data did not establish that interpretation: A/B/C were different model executions, and a new run can succeed without source attachment.

The original conditional rate was retained as descriptive evidence. It was no longer treated as a population performance decline or an individually identified causal failure fraction.

## 2. Check the environment and the measurement

A trajectory audit raised network access as a potential confound, leading to a separate isolated Developer collection. It did not isolate network access as the sole cause of any collection difference.

An evaluator audit then reproduced selected archived scores and tested implementation sensitivity. Renaming local identifiers in two gold implementations preserved functional success but changed original constraint scores. This showed a limitation of the measurement, not proof that all failed candidates were correct.

All 30 task rules were reviewed; 10 revised rules were validated and frozen. Every old isolated-study patch was rescored under the alternative evaluator, while original scores were preserved. This was measurement sensitivity work, not new model data.

## 3. Add the missing ordinary retry

The prospective design introduced B0 as an independent handoff-only screen and B1 as a fresh identical-prompt execution. C added source requirements. All roles ran on every fixed handoff, with randomized order.

B1 could therefore reveal fresh-run success without the intervention. The whole-bank C-minus-B1 contrast was primary; the B0-failure subset was secondary and never fed results into implementation.

## 4. Keep implementation failures distinct from scientific failures

The first adapter forwarded an SDK output-limit parameter without converting it to the provider-native spelling. A rejected response exposed that the intended 8,192-token bound had not been established. All pre-fix attempts, including ten completed runs, were archived together and excluded regardless of score. The corrected cohort restarted the original 270-role schedule.

Six later transport-invalid attempts were separately archived and retried under the recorded rule. Twelve valid resource-limit exits stayed in the final 270-run denominator. Unknown calls retained conservative charges. The initial freeze and amended source versions were preserved.

This history matters because an experiment can have a sensible design and still require implementation auditing before its outcomes are interpretable.

## 5. Report the result the control actually supports

B1 and C both achieved 66/90 revised joint successes. The primary difference was 0.0 percentage points, with a 95% task-bootstrap interval from −7.8 to +8.9. The result established neither superiority nor equivalence.

The study ended at its fixed target rather than adding runs until a favorable result appeared. Its contribution is a bounded, reproducible investigation of outcome discordance and measurement validity, with an appropriate source-versus-retry comparison—not a claim to have identified all transmission or execution causes.
